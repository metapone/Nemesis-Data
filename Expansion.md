# Achievements

## 5 Achievement cards

| Title | Requirement | Effect |
|-------|-------------|--------|
| Elixir of Life | Be the first Character to use Laboratory Room Action. | Discard this card during your Round to heal 1 Serious Wound (even not Dressed) or all Light Wounds of your Character.<br>In addition you may remove 1 Contamination card from your hand (without Scanning it). |
| Hard Reset | Be the first Character to use the Generator Room Action. | Discard (before Time marker is on any blue space) during your Round to replace current Coordinates card with one of your choosing. |
| Hunter | Be the first Character to kill an Adult Intruder. | Discard this card during your Round to deal 2 Injuries to an Intruder in the Room you are in. Check Injury Effect. |
| Jury-Rigged | Be the first Character to use Demolition in the Storage Room. | Discard this card during your Round to take any Craft Item from blue Item deck. |
| Setting the Trap | Be the first Character to Send the Signal. | Discard when a Character or Intruder enters the Comms Room to deal a Serious Wound or 2 Injuries to everyone in the Comms Room. |

All Achievement cards have the following text on the back:

>During Setup, draw 2 Achievement cards and place them face-up next to the main board.
>
>Players may look at these cards at any moment.
>
>The first player who meets the requirement described in the upper part of the Achievement card gains that card and place it in their inventory.
>
>The lower part of the card describes its effect and when it can be played.
